PERSONAL RESEARCH PROJECT

POD Market Research

A personal research project focused on print-on-demand market opportunities in the United States.

Project Purpose

The project aims to support product niche research, competitor analysis, and evaluation of market opportunities using publicly available marketplace data.

Planned Features

  • Research Etsy product listings
  • Compare publicly listed prices
  • Analyze competing product offers
  • Organize market research findings
  • Support evidence-based POD decisions

Technology

The project is being developed using n8n workflow automation and planned Etsy Open API integration.

Access

This is a personal research tool. It is not offered as a public service.

This project is independent and is not affiliated with Etsy.
